Privacy Policy

Moonrise Mini Golf, published by Moonrise Studios · Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version. We don't sell your data, show ads or run analytics, and we have no servers of our own. Playing solo sends nothing to us. To let you play online with friends, the game uses Meta's platform (your Meta name, an ID Meta makes for this game, and Meta's invites so friends can join you) and Unity's online services (an anonymous player ID, your IP address and, if you use it, live voice). Voice goes live to the players in your game and is never recorded or stored by us. You can ask us to delete your data at any time, wherever you live, for free.

Who we are

"Moonrise Studios", "we" and "us" means the publisher of Moonrise Mini Golf (the "game"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. For questions, or to use any of your rights, email moonrisestudiovr@gmail.com.

We don't collect your real name, email address, location, contacts or payment details. We don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we don't build profiles about you. The game is free and has no purchases.

Service providers

  • Meta Platforms Technologies: the Meta Horizon Store, the Meta Platform SDK (entitlement, identity, blocking, presence, invites and deep links, and the store's rate-and-review window) and Meta's user-reporting tools. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Technologies: Unity Authentication (the anonymous player ID), Unity's multiplayer session and Relay services (online games) and Vivox (voice chat). The game doesn't send Unity hardware statistics or any other device information beyond what these online services need to connect you. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. We share data with them only to run the features described above. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety.

Why we're allowed to use it

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on:

  • providing the service you asked for: online play, voice chat and showing your name to the people you play with;
  • legitimate interests: keeping play safe, handling reports and blocks, and making sure the game was obtained from the store;
  • your consent: the microphone.

You can withdraw consent at any time by muting, turning the microphone off in the game, or turning off the microphone permission for the game in your headset's settings.

How long data is kept

  • Game data, voice, names and IDs shared during a game: only while the game is running. We keep nothing afterwards, and voice is never stored.
  • Players you met this session: kept in the game's memory until you close it, so you can still report them. Never written to disk.
  • Anonymous online player ID: kept by Unity until it is deleted (see below).
  • Reports: kept by Meta, and by us if we receive one, for as long as needed to review and act on it, then deleted.
  • Settings, bests and fit data: on your headset until you uninstall the game.

How to delete your data

Anyone can ask us to delete their data, wherever they live, free of charge. Email moonrisestudiovr@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data", and tell us your Meta username and roughly when you played online.

We hold no player data on servers of our own, so there is nothing of yours to delete on our side. We will ask Unity to delete the online data processed for the game (your anonymous player ID and anything linked to it), and point you to Meta for data Meta holds. We reply within 30 days. If something can't be deleted, for example because the law requires it to be kept, we'll tell you why.

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your headset, including the sign-in for your anonymous online player ID. Your Meta account data is managed by Meta: see Meta's privacy settings and tools.

Your other rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or port your data, or to object to or restrict how it's used. Email us to use any of these rights. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the online features, email us and we'll have the related data deleted.

Security

We keep data to a minimum and hold none on servers of our own. Our providers protect the data they process with industry-standard measures. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us if you spot a problem.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, say so in the game's store listing or release notes.
