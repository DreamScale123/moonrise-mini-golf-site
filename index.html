Start hereThe Moonrise Clubhouse

A lantern-lit terrace over a twilight sea, with the golf-ball moon rising between two portals. A two-minute tutorial on the practice greens teaches you to putt, move and bank a shot. Pick your ball, warm up on the practice green, meet friends, then walk up to a portal and play.

36 holesGolden hour to moonrise

Two worlds, eighteen holes each. Every hole teaches one idea, has an honest safe route and a real hole-in-one line, and is a place worth standing in.

Course one · par 49Lantern Cove

A golden-hour seaside cove. Bank off a giant conch, putt along stilt-village boardwalks and through a glowing sea-glass grotto, send your ball down pipes and past a shipwreck, and finish under the lighthouse at the Lantern Heart. An ace sets off fireworks over the bay.

Course two · par 46Neon Shroom Forest

Night in a hushed, bioluminescent forest under a huge moon. Roll through root tunnels and down a log flume, past chiming crystal caps and glowing mushrooms, and climb the towering Mother Shroom for the finale.

Made for your handsPutting that feels real

Real ball physics

Simulated 360 times a second: soft taps stay precise, banks, slopes and pipes behave.

A putter that fits

A classic blade putter you can fit to your grip. Squeeze to arm it and swing; let go for practice swings.

Play together

Up to four friends online with a join code, spatial voice chat, and scores on your wrist watch.

Move your way

Teleport or walk, snap or smooth turn with a comfort vignette, warp to your ball, seated mode, and left- or right-handed play.

Friendly by designYour round, your people

Games are private: friends join with a short code, and nobody is matched with strangers. Voices come from each player's avatar, and you stay in control.

  • Mute anyone for yourself, instantly.
  • Block through Meta's own block tool.
  • Report a player from the in-game menu or the Meta menu.
  • Voice is never recorded by us.
