Start hereThe Moonrise Clubhouse

A lantern-lit terrace over a twilight sea, with the golf-ball moon rising behind a row of portals. A two-minute tutorial on the practice greens teaches you to putt, move and bank a shot. Pick your ball, warm up on the practice green, meet friends, then walk up to a portal and play.

Every dayA new daily challenge

A new challenge waits in the clubhouse every day, from Closest to the Pin to Ace Hunt, on a hole from one of the courses. Three tries, your best counts, and your streak grows with every day you play.

72 free holesFour worlds to putt through

Four free worlds, eighteen holes each, plus our add-on courses, Glowtown and the new Dinosaur Valley. Every hole has an honest safe route and a real hole-in-one line, and is a place worth standing in.

Course oneLantern Cove

A golden-hour seaside cove. Bank off a giant conch, putt along stilt-village boardwalks and through a glowing sea-glass grotto, send your ball down pipes and past a shipwreck, and finish under the lighthouse at the Lantern Heart. An ace sets off fireworks over the bay.

Course twoNeon Shroom Forest

Night in a hushed, bioluminescent forest under a huge moon. Roll through root tunnels and down a log flume, past chiming crystal caps and glowing mushrooms, and climb the towering Mother Shroom for the finale.

Course threePumpkin Hollow

A cozy Halloween night under the harvest moon. Cross the covered bridge, putt into a witch's cauldron, find the manor's secret passage and finish inside the Great Jack as lantern drones light the sky.

Course fourAtomic Skyways

Sunny days in the city of tomorrow. Putt through the Sparkle-Jet Car Wash, across a saucer skating rink and into the Stardome, then watch the Starliner lift off on 18.

Add-on courseGlowtown

A neon showbiz city at night. Putt past the Leaning Motel, work the flippers on a sky-arcade pinball table and drop off the top of the tower. A $3.99 add-on (everyone who played a round in its launch week keeps it).

New add-on courseDinosaur Valley

A green river valley on a prehistoric morning, full of friendly dinosaurs. Thread the iguanodon, ride the geysers and the mud luge, and finish high on the volcano as it erupts. Free through November 1, and every round finished by then keeps it; after that it's a $3.99 add-on.

Made for your handsPutting that feels real

Real ball physics

Simulated 360 times a second: soft taps stay precise, banks, slopes and pipes behave.

A putter that fits

A classic blade putter you can fit to your grip. Squeeze to arm it and swing; let go for practice swings.

Play together

Up to four friends online with a join code, spatial voice chat, and scores on your wrist watch.

Move your way

Teleport or walk, snap or smooth turn with a comfort vignette, warp to your ball, seated mode, and left- or right-handed play.

Friendly by designYour round, your people

Games are private: friends join with a short code, and nobody is matched with strangers. Voices come from each player's avatar, and you stay in control.

  • Mute anyone for yourself, instantly.
  • Block through Meta's own block tool.
  • Report a player from the in-game menu or the Meta menu.
  • Voice is never recorded by us.
